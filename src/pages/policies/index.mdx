Standing orders for anyone filing a Briefing or a Dispatch. Read this once at
the start of the semester; every assessment brief links back to it rather than
repeating it.

## Late submissions

A Field Dispatch or Peace Conference submission lands late loses 5% of the
available marks per day, to a maximum of five days, after which it is not
accepted without a prior extension. The network does not wait for stragglers —
neither should you.

## Extensions

Apply before the deadline, not after. Illness, misadventure and equipment
failure are considered on their evidence, not on the strength of the prose
describing them. A request made after the fact is treated as a late
submission with an explanation attached, not an extension.

## Academic integrity

Every claimed biological phenomenon must trace to a real source — a textbook,
a paper, a review — cited in the body text or a closing reference list.
Fabricated citations are treated as fabricated data, because in this course
they are the data. Generative tools may assist drafting, but the diplomatic
metaphor and the underlying biology must both be understood well enough to
defend without the tool present.

## Getting help

Office hours are held weekly; the sign-up link is circulated with the first
Briefing of semester and is not repeated here, since dossiers this old go
stale. Outside office hours, route questions through the course forum so the
answer reaches everyone running the same case, not just the one who asked.
