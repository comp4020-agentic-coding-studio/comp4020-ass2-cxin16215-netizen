Tobias Lindqvist spent five years mediating supply contracts before retraining
in fungal ecology, and has not stopped noticing the overlap. He runs the
weekly Briefings as a working seminar: bring a claim from the reading, and
leave having either sharpened it or watched it fail under questioning — both
outcomes count as a good session.

No photograph is on file for this entry, for the same reason as the
convenor's: this course has no image-generation tool available, and an
invented likeness is not a substitute worth having.
