Marguerite Osei trained as a field mycologist before this course existed, and
still thinks the diplomatic framing is a useful provocation rather than a
finding. Her running condition for keeping the metaphor in the course is that
every week it must be checked against the literature it is dressed up as
describing — the day it stops earning that check, it gets cut, not qualified.

No photograph is on file for this entry. The dossier motif is deliberate: this
course does not have image generation available to it, and faking a portrait
would be a worse choice than saying so plainly.
