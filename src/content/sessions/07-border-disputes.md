## Before the session

Read the assigned material on fungal genet boundaries. Bring one guess at
what "recognition" could mean for an organism with no nervous system.

## In the session

Distinguish three things that get conflated in casual accounts: a visible
fungal fruiting body, a genet, and a mycelial network. Map which of this
week's claims apply to which.

## Afterwards

Carry the recognition question into week 10, where the same boundary problem
gets a specific genetic mechanism attached to it.
