## Before the session

Reread your notes from weeks 1, 3 and 8 — the credentials test and its two
clearest failure cases. Bring a ranked list: which week's label was the most
honest, which the least.

## In the session

Run the full audit as a class, week by week, in the order taught. Every
label defended has to survive a challenge from someone who read the same
material and disagrees.

## Afterwards

The Peace Conference assessment asks you to write this same audit alone, for
a phenomenon of your own choosing, held to the same standard set in week 1.
See the [assessment page](/assessments/) for the brief.
