## Before the session

Read the assigned case material on garlic mustard invasion. Bring a view on
whether "broken treaty" requires an original two-party agreement, and
whether this case actually has one.

## In the session

Trace the chain: invader arrives, produces compound, native fungi suppressed,
native plants lose a nutrient partner, invader gains ground. Identify which
link in that chain is best supported and which is weakest.

## Afterwards

Compare this week's one-directional case against week 6's double agent — one
phenomenon has a clear external saboteur, the other doesn't.
