## Before the session

Read the assigned material on juglone and on mycorrhizosphere antibiosis.
Bring one candidate answer to what, if anything, is being "sanctioned" in
each case, and who benefits.

## In the session

Compare a sanction (a threat meant to change behaviour) with a chemical
weapon (an effect that does not require the target to notice it coming). Sort
this week's two phenomena into one category or the other, or both.

## Afterwards

Note whether either phenomenon requires the attacker to know it has a target
at all — this is the same question week 9 returns to at a longer timescale.
