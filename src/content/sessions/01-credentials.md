## Before the session

Read the assigned primer on mycorrhizal structure. Bring one claim about "the
wood wide web" you have picked up outside class — a documentary, a headline,
a conversation. You are not defending it. You are putting it on the table.

## In the session

We build the credentials test together: for a plant-fungus pairing to earn
the word "network" in this course, what has to be shown? Movement of carbon
or nutrients, not just fungal presence. More than one host connected through
a shared fungal individual, not just the same species growing nearby. Every
later week's diplomatic label has to clear this bar before it is allowed to
sit on top of a real phenomenon.

## Afterwards

The test agreed here is the standing check for the rest of semester. Refer
back to it before accepting any week's metaphor at face value — including the
convenor's.
