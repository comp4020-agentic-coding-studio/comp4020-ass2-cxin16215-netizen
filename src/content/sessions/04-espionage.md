## Before the session

Read the assigned material on Monotropa and on orchid seed germination. Bring
an answer to: is a mycoheterotroph parasitising the fungus, the tree, or
both?

## In the session

Work through what the fungus is actually losing in each case, and to whom.
"Espionage" implies a target that has something taken from it — name the
target precisely.

## Afterwards

Keep this week's case in mind for week 6, where the same fungal partner can
be a legitimate trader with one host and something closer to this week's
freeloader with another.
