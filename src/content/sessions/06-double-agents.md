## Before the session

Bring your Field Dispatch case in draft: one documented fungal partner,
identified across at least two different plant hosts or conditions, where its
role shifts.

## In the session

Workshop drafts in pairs. The question for every draft: what specifically
changes — light, nutrient status, host identity — and is that enough to call
it a switch in loyalty, or just a switch in outcome?
