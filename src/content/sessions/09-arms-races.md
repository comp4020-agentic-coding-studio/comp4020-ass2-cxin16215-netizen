## Before the session

Reread your week-4 or week-6 notes with one question in mind: what would the
fungus need to evolve to stop this from happening, and is there evidence it
has?

## In the session

Build the coevolutionary story explicitly, step by step, and mark which
steps are observed directly and which are inferred from present-day defence
mechanisms alone.

## Afterwards

Keep the observed/inferred distinction from this week active for the rest of
semester — it recurs in week 11's case study and in the Peace Conference.
