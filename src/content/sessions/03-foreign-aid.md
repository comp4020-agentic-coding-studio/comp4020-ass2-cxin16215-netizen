## Before the session

Read the assigned excerpt on Douglas-fir/paper birch carbon transfer and one
piece of the critical response it later drew. Note every sentence in either
piece that claims more than the data in it supports.

## In the session

Draw a line down the middle of the board: measured claim on one side, popular
addition on the other. Every phrase you brought goes on one side or the
other, out loud, with a reason.

## Afterwards

This week is the sharpest test yet of the credentials rule from week 1. If
"aid" survives the line-drawing exercise, say exactly what work it is still
doing once the overclaiming is removed.
