## Outline

- when two hyphae from the same or a compatible genet meet, they can fuse
  (anastomose), sharing cytoplasm and nuclei
- when they are incompatible, recognition genes at het loci — studied in
  detail in fungi such as Neurospora crassa and Podospora anserina — trigger
  rejection, sometimes localised cell death at the contact point, stopping
  fusion before it happens
- read against week 7: this is a finer-grained, genetically specified
  version of the same border question — not just who wins a patch of soil,
  but who is permitted to merge with whom at all
- why "détente" is the closest of this semester's borrowed terms to literal:
  fusion is a real, bounded cooperative event between otherwise separate
  individuals, gated by an identifiable recognition mechanism — closer to an
  actual détente than most of this course's metaphors get to be
