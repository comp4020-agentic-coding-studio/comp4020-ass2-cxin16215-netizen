## Outline

- the same fungal species, and sometimes the same individual, can behave as a
  net mutualist with one plant and a net drain on another, depending on
  partner identity and environmental context such as light or soil nutrient
  status
- this ties directly back to week 2's biological market and weeks 4–5's
  cheating and defence: partner-fidelity feedback, the same sanctioning logic
  behind the labelling experiments, is one mechanism that keeps a partner
  from sliding permanently into the exploitative role
- "double agent" earns its keep here more than most weeks' labels: there is
  a real, measured behavioural switch tied to identifiable conditions, not
  just an observer's choice of framing
- the Field Dispatch asks you to find and argue one such case yourself,
  against the same evidentiary standard used in this lecture
