## Outline

- if cheating symbionts (week 4) and context-dependent exploitation (week 6)
  impose a real cost, selection should favour hosts and fungi able to detect
  and punish it — this is the evolutionary logic behind the sanctioning
  mechanisms introduced in weeks 2 and 6
- orchid-fungus specificity is one place this plays out: how narrowly or
  broadly a fungal partner associates with orchid lineages tracks, in part,
  the cost of being exploited by a given lineage
- what "arms race" buys: a reason to expect detection and punishment
  mechanisms exist at all, without needing intent on either side
- what it costs: an arms race is usually inferred from present-day defences,
  not observed as a sequence of escalating moves over time. Say that plainly
  rather than implying a single semester's reading watched an escalation
  happen
