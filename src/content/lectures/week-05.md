## Outline

- allelopathy: black walnut's juglone suppresses growth in many neighbouring
  plants — a chemical border enforced without any agreement between the
  parties on either side of it
- ectomycorrhizal fungi also produce antibiotic and antifungal compounds in
  the zone of soil they occupy around a root, the mycorrhizosphere,
  suppressing competing fungi and some plant pathogens there
- read as sanctions: this protects an existing trade partnership (week 2)
  from a third party, without any negotiation between attacker and target
- limit of the metaphor: a sanction in statecraft is a communicated threat
  meant to change behaviour before it is carried out. A fungal antibiotic
  communicates nothing — it is chemistry that happens to have that effect,
  whether or not the target could ever have responded to a warning
