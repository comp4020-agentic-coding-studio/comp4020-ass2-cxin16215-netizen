## Outline

- fire, drought and logging remove hosts and change soil chemistry, and the
  surviving fungal community shifts in composition, not only in size
- some ectomycorrhizal taxa are fire-adapted early colonisers of
  regenerating seedlings; others disappear with their host and do not return
  quickly
- surviving networks reallocate: carbon and nutrients concentrate toward
  whichever hosts remain connected — a redistribution along existing links,
  not a rescue directed at whoever needs it most
- limit of the metaphor: "crisis" and "displacement" describe the pattern
  accurately; they do not license the claim that the network is tracking
  need, any more than week 3's "aid" did
