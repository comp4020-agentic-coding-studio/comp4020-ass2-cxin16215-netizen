## Outline

- revisit the week 1 credentials test against everything since: did every
  week's phenomenon actually clear it, or did some get a pass because the
  label was doing rhetorical work the evidence alone wasn't
- the audit, briefly: most durable framing across the semester — week 2's
  biological market, week 10's détente, both gated by a real, checkable
  mechanism; most strained — week 3's aid and this week's own "conference,"
  since soil fungi are not convening anything, we are
- the point of building a bold metaphor for twelve weeks was never to prove
  it works. It was to build something specific enough to fail informatively
  when it doesn't, in a place you can point to
- The Peace Conference assessment asks you to run this same audit for a
  phenomenon of your own choosing, against the same standard applied here
