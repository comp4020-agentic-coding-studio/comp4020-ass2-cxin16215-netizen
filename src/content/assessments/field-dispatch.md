## The brief

> File a dispatch on one fungal partner caught operating as a double agent —
> mutualist to one host, exploiter of another — and say exactly what evidence
> earns it that label.

Pick a case covered in weeks 1–6, or a documented case of your own finding
that clears the credentials test from week 1. State the two roles the fungus
plays, the specific factor that shifts it between them (host identity,
light, nutrient status), and the strongest evidence for the shift. Then say,
in your own voice, whether "double agent" is doing honest work here or
borrowing more drama than the biology supports — this course expects that
judgement stated, not avoided.

## What you submit

A dispatch of 800–1200 words, written as a case file: the case, the
evidence, and your verdict on the metaphor. Cite your sources by name and
venue in the text; a fabricated citation is treated as fabricated evidence,
not a formatting slip.

## Diplomatic accuracy (60%)

Whether the case genuinely clears the week 1 credentials test, whether the
mutualist/exploiter shift is backed by a real, named mechanism rather than
assertion, and whether your verdict on the metaphor is argued rather than
asserted.

## Clarity of dispatch (40%)

Whether a reader who has not taken this course could follow the case,
distinguish measured claims from your own inference, and understand your
verdict without needing to ask you.
