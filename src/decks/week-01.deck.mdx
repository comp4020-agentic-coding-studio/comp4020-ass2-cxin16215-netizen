# Underground Diplomacy

## Week 1: Credentials

---

## Two associations, one word

- **Arbuscular (AMF):** fungus enters the root cell wall, branches into an
  arbuscule — most land plant species carry one
- **Ectomycorrhizal (ECM):** fungus stays outside the cell wall, forms a
  Hartig net and a sheath — dominant in temperate and boreal forest trees

Both get called "mycorrhizal networks." Neither is automatically a network.

---

# What earns the word "network"?

Not proximity. Not the same species nearby. **Measured transfer, between
named hosts, through a shared fungal individual.**

---

## The credentials test

1. Is there a shared fungal individual, not just the same species?
2. Is there measured carbon or nutrient movement between hosts through it?
3. Does the claim survive without the word "communicate" doing the work?

Every phenomenon this semester has to clear this before its diplomatic label
is allowed to sit on top of it.
